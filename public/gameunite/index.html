Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. GameUnite is free software under the GPL-3.0. There is no paid tier, no ads and no tracking, and there never will be.

Does it come with games?

GameUnite does not download, provide or manage games, ROMs or BIOS files. It only launches what you already own.

Which emulators does it install?

19 open-source emulators and the RetroArch cores, downloaded from their official releases and checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes. When an emulator's license forbids redistribution, GameUnite guides you to its official download instead.

Do I need ES-DE, EmuDeck or anything else first?

No. GameUnite recognises your games on its own. If you already use ES-DE, RetroBat, LaunchBox, Pegasus or Playnite, you can import your library, and keep it in sync with ES-DE if you like.

Will it mess up my emulator settings?

Every key it writes is tracked, a value you changed by hand is left alone, and gameunite --release puts the original files back.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I install it?

The installers are not code-signed yet (certificates cost money and GameUnite is free). On Windows choose “More info” then “Run anyway”; on macOS, “Open Anyway” in Privacy & Security, once.

Is it finished?

It is a public beta (0.5.0-rc.1). Windows, macOS and Android are tested on real hardware; Linux is tested in a container and the Steam Deck not yet. Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub.

Does it collect data?

No. It only connects to the network for what you use: update checks, the scraper, your store accounts and emulator downloads.

Can I help?

Yes! Twelve of the fourteen languages were translated without a native speaker: reading one is the most useful thing you can do. Code, themes and bug reports are welcome too.
